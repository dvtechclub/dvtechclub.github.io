Technology Club Constitution

The official DVHS Tech Club Constitution.

Article I: Name and Purpose

Section 1: Name

- The name of this organization shall be the "Technology Club," or alternatively as the “Tech Club”, or “DV Tech Club”.

Section 2: Purpose

- The purpose of this club is to allow Deer Valley High School students to have a place to further explore and participate in technology related activities, as well as provide an environment to further learning and skills in the field of technology. The Club aims to invoke innovation, inspiration and collaboration to those passionate about technology.

Article II: Membership

Section 1: Eligibility

- Membership is open to all DVHS students regardless of their prior knowledge, experience, or skill. The club welcomes all students.

Section 2: Rights and Responsibilities

- Members have the right to attend all Club meetings, events, and activities and to participate in the Club's decision-making processes.

- Members are required to attend at minimum 2 meetings per month in order to be considered an active member.

- Members are encouraged to actively contribute to club discussions, propose ideas, initiate projects, and partake in various technological endeavors.

'

- Members are expected to conduct themselves respectfully, supportively, and inclusively towards fellow members and the broader school community. Discrimination, harassment, and any form of negativity will not be tolerated.

Article III: Leadership and Officers

Section 1: Executive Officers

- The Club shall have the following executive officers: President, Media Officer, Vice President, Productivity Officer, as well as a general role of board member.

- The minimum & recommended number of officers shall be 4 but is not limited as any individual who shows great interest and passion for the club and technology should be offered the opportunity to be a member of the officer board even if not given a declared role.

- All board members have an equal vote in officer decisions no matter what role or position they have within the board.

Section 2: Duties of Executive Officers

- President: The President shall lead Club meetings, represent the Club in official matters, and serve as a source of inspiration and guidance for Club members. The president shall also plan meetings and try to make agendas for each meeting to guide members of what to do.

- Vice President: The Vice President shall work closely with the President, assist in organizing Club activities, and assume the President's responsibilities when needed.

- Media Officer: The media officer shall be responsible for the PR and outreach of the club, including but not limited to social media and poster/flyer design. Media Officers will also be responsible for designing club media and documents.

- Productivity Officer: The Productivity officer shall assume the role of secretary and keep a record of all club activities and also research and look out for events and activities for the club to participate in such as hackathons, game jams or other events.

Section 2: Election of New Officers

- The election of a new officer board will take place at the end of the school year and the electees shall consist of no seniors as they will no longer be present in the following years which is when the new officer board should assume responsibility of the club.

- For the election process of new officers, first ask all members, or current non-senior officers, if anyone is interested in becoming an officer for next year. Then, the current standing officers will decide on at least 4 new officer board members (NOTE: A current board member rerunning cannot vote or decide on themselves becoming an officer again) these individuals will then be offered to become board members.

- Upon the election of the new board members, the board must come to a unanimous decision on who will take each executive board role. Any remaining officers will still be an officer board member.
