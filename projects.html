Projects

Here is a collection of some of the projects that we have worked on here at Tech Club.

2024: NASA Artemis

Throughout the 2024-2025 school year, members from the DVHS Tech Club participated in the NASA App Challenge.

The group was able to produce a Matplotlib visual representation/simulation of the flightpath of NASA's Artemis II mission to the moon. The team took raw data from NASA and processed the flightpaths and demonstrated their skills in physics and math to create an accurate simulation of the spacecraft's trajectory.

Created by Nathan Narrik, Patrick Lester

2025: OpenX 1.3.1

OpenX is a Python application that lets users render objects in a 3D envionrenment, and provides an easier way to utilize OpenGL within PyGame.

Users can create N64/PS1-esque 3D scenes with custom textures and geometry. Above are example of scences: a bouncing ball and an interior of a building. Multiple features include keyboard reading for input, camera controls, skyboxes, and custom model files.

See the bouncing ball demo in CodeHS!

Interested in future relases of OpenX? Visit AMWonline for more info.

OpenX was created in Python by Madden Wilkins.

2025: BiteByte

In October of 2025, Representative Abraham Hamadeh participated to have DVHS' district, AZ-08, be eligible for the Congressional App Challenge. The Congressional App Challenge is a national challenge for students to create any app of their choosing. With a tight deadline on October 30, we created a calorie tracking app named BiteByte.

BiteByte allows users to input their height, weight, age, gender, and acitivity level: once done, the app will show users how many calories they need in a day to healithy gain, lose, or maintain their weight.

Run BiteByte in your browser!

BiteByte was created in React Native by Ye Lynn Kyaw, Jason Nguyen, Koen Chambers and Madden Wilkins.

2022-2026: skDOS 2

skDOS 2 is an ongoing project started in 2022 by Madden Wilkins and carried into DVHS Tech Club in 2025.

It features a command line interface and lets users explore a virtual operating system like environment that can perform tasks like file management, editing, image viewing and more all in a single text-based window. users can even write their own programs using a dedicated language called Delta. skDOS stands for Simulated Kernel Disk Operating System, as it started out resembling regular DOS.

While skDOS started as a fun project to mimic a CLI interface, like that of DOS, it has become into a versatile tool to not only challenge yourself in what you can make in a limited environment -- but also help teach people not familiar with CLI interfaces some basics.

Run skDOS v2.64 Second Compact Edition in your browser!

Visit AMWonline for more details on skDOS.

skDOS 2 was created by Madden Wilkins in Python (with help from Koen Chambers).

2026: Grade Calculator

Our grade calculator lets you take your grades in PowerSchool and see both what your grade is unrounded, and play with your grades to see what you need on tests, or test possibilities, etc.

Just simply copy and paste from PowerSchool to see your grade breakdown.
