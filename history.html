History

A year-by-year look at how the DVHS Tech Club got here.

2022–2023

Tech Club founder Nathan Narrik.

Tech club was founded by Nathan Narrik in 2022 to inspire the world of technology to students at Deer Valley High School. This year was building the foundation for future tech club members and learning process.

2023–2024

This year was the most successful year in terms of members. We gained several new members who even soon became leaders of the club, the brilliant Ye Lynn Kyaw, and Jason Nguyen. Due to the increase of members, King Nathan Narrik began instructing kids in Computer Science related classes, like AP Computer Science A to guarantee 4s and 5s. Regardless of the workload of new members Nathan Narrik managed to get 2nd place in the renowned Congressional App Challenge where students in each district compete for the best community app.

2024–2025

During this year we had a clear goal, “Work on the NASA Project”. We had our eye on this project ever since the beginning of tech club and this was the year we dedicated ourselves to working on it. Nathan Narrik and friends were able to push themselves and reached an astounding semi-finals. Along with the NASA project, sensei Nathan Narrik aided his pupils to the Congressional App Challenge. The pupils did not win, but they gained valuable experience. To expand tech club arduinos (circuits, robotics) were also introduced to create a wide variety of activities each member can participate in.

2025–2026

Unfortunately our glorious Nathan Narrik left the year leaving it to the new leaders Madden Wilkins, Jason Nguyen, Koen Chambers, and Ye Lynn Kyaw. Our new leaders sought organization and connection. We made the ease of the access and communication easier and brainstormed ideas to make sure everyone can participate. To honor Nathan Narrik, our team attempted the Congressional App Challenge and didn’t win, but it was for the experience and fun we got from it. Many tech club members this year made several apps and programs like the Grade Calculator, skDOS 2, OpenX, and BiteByte.
