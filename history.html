History

A year-by-year look at how the DVHS Tech Club got here.

2022–2023

Tech Club founder Nathan Narrik.

Tech club was founded by Nathan Narrik in 2022 to inspire the world of technology to students at Deer Valley High School. This year was focused on building the foundation for future tech club members and learning processes.

2023–2024

This year was the most successful year in terms of members and general productivity. We gained several new members who even soon became future leaders of the club, such as current officers Ye Lynn Kyaw and Jason Nguyen. Due to the increase of members, Narrik began instructing kids in Computer Science related classes, such as AP Computer Science A to guarantee scores of 4s and 5s. Regardless of the workload that comes with an influx of new members, Narrik managed to achieve 2nd place in the nationally renowned Congressional App Challenge, where students in each house district compete for the best community app.

2024–2025

During this year we had a clear goal, “Work on the NASA Project”. We had our eye on this project ever since the beginning of the Tech Club -- and this was the year we dedicated ourselves to working on it. Narrik, along with other members such as Patrick Lester, were able to push themselves and reached an astounding place within the semi-finals. Along with the NASA Artemis project, Narrik aided fellow club members to attempt the Congressional App Challenge. Although they did not win, they gained valuable experience in the deployment cycle of a fully fledged mobile app. To expand Tech Club's reach in all areas of the meaning of the word "Tech", the club started working on new projects. These included things such as Arduinos (circuits and robotics), which were also introduced to create a wide variety of activities each member can participate in.

2025–2026

Unfortunately, with Nathan Narrik having graduated in the class of 2025, Tech Club was left to it's current officers (whom are all listed on our 'Resources' page). Our new leaders sought more organization and connection. In order to accomplish that goal, we made the ease of the access and communication easier for new members, and brainstormed ideas to make sure everyone can participate. To honor Nathan Narrik, our team attempted the Congressional App Challenge. Although we didn’t win, it was still fulfilling in the sense of new experience and having fun while coding. Many Tech club members this year made several apps and programs: such as the Grade Calculator, skDOS 2, OpenX, and BiteByte -- of which can all be found on our "Projects" page.
